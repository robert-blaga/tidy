# Philosophy

Agents don't necessarily need smarter models to become more useful. Often they need better memory systems.

For filing, the missing piece is not another classifier. It is durable taste: where *you* put books, invoices, screenshots, drafts, and odd little files that only make sense in the context of your machine.

Tidy turns that taste into a small markdown wiki the agent can read, follow, and improve.

The pattern is bigger than filing. Tidy ships knowing one domain (filing) because it's the most universal first use case — but the architecture is domain-neutral. You can teach Tidy new domains (inbox triage, customer research, copy drafting, whatever you keep asking for). Each new domain accumulates its own skills, with your approval at every step.

- durable local memory
- readable by humans
- executable by agents
- updated with approval
- portable across runtimes
- extensible across domains, not locked to one kind of work

The wiki is the durable part. Skills are case law; domains are constitutional amendments; the runtime is replaceable.
